Initial release of `@motiro/types` — a collection of TypeScript type utilities.

Includes:

- **Number** — `NumberSafe`, `Numeric`, `Zero`
- **Object** — `Dict`, `Merge`, `PartialBy`, `PartialDeep`, `PartialExcept`, `PartialNullable`, `Prettify`, `PrettifyDeep`, `RequiredDeep`, `ValueOf`, `Writable`
- **String** — `Alphanumeric`, `Digit`, `Letter`, `LowercaseLetter`, `UppercaseLetter`, `Whitespace`
- **Utility** — `Branded`, `LiteralUnion`, `Maybe`, `Nullable`, `Optional`
